Atomic-Stockfish browser release
================================

Release: https://github.com/Belzedar94/Atomic-Stockfish/releases/tag/browser-wasm-01cc6f4

Corresponding source: https://github.com/Belzedar94/Atomic-Stockfish/tree/01cc6f44f52709d3f4a0b0994d68f9226cc311fa

These browser artifacts are the official `browser-wasm-01cc6f4` release. The exact compiler,
flags, source inventory, artifact sizes, and hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`; the
release-provided checksums are preserved in `SHA256SUMS`.

The NNUE file named in `SHA256SUMS` is hosted separately rather than copied into this directory.
It is verified by the worker before use with SHA-256
`99dc67eabf26a64faeeca3a88b4c38597a840b8d4a874b9f2cf658c6f92a04a6`.
